# Attributions

The Matcha Flavoured Field Wiki is an unofficial community companion. It
reorganizes recipe data, release notes, names, models, and textures so players
can explore the pack in a browser. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Mojang, Microsoft, Modrinth, or any of the people credited below.

## Matcha Flavoured

- **Klei** created Matcha Flavoured and, according to the credits bundled with
  release 1.03, made the pack's textures and data except where otherwise noted.
  The original project is available on
  [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/datapack/matcha-flavoured).
- **Ciren** composed the “Golden” music disc. The pack notes that the disc was
  added in memory of Dakota.

Matcha Flavoured is distributed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). This wiki
is a transformed presentation of that material: it extracts and reorganizes
pack data, generates searchable entries, and presents recipes and textures in a
new interface.

## Credits carried forward from the pack

The original pack credits thank the following people and projects for
inspiration or technical frameworks. The credits also make clear that these
people do not know of or endorse Matcha Flavoured.

- **Raspmary**: the
  [Raspberry Flavoured](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/raspberry-flavoured)
  modpack inspired parts of Matcha Flavoured's progression, resources, and
  alloys.
- **JermsyBoy**: the
  [A Little Taste of Jerm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/a-little-taste-of-jerm)
  resource pack inspired Matcha Flavoured's visual direction. JermsyBoy's
  [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCICnSwa3x5sHTWmTP6YO30w)
  is also linked in the pack credits.
- **MiziraGamez**: the
  [Repair Cost Remover](https://modrinth.com/datapack/repair-cost-remover)
  datapack provided a framework for endless repairing.
- **Gooporeal**: the
  [Gooporeal's Lichen](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/gooporeals-lichen)
  resource pack inspired the lichen remodels.
- **Vectorwing**: the
  [Detail Brush](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/detail-brush)
  resource pack inspired many textures.
- **Rhythmic and PurpurMC**: the
  [Looting Shears](https://modrinth.com/datapack/purpurpacks-looting-shears)
  datapack provided a framework for looting shears.
